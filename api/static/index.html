Prescription Sequence Symmetry Analysis (PSSA) Methodology

PSSA identifies adverse drug reaction cascades by assessing directional asymmetry in the order patients initiate pairs of medications:

Directional Sequence A: Drug A ➔ Drug B (n_AB) Candidate Cascade
Day 0: Drug A Started
→
ADR Side Effect
→
Drug B Prescribed

Suspected cascade: Drug B is initiated to treat an unrecognised adverse reaction caused by Drug A.

Directional Sequence B: Drug B ➔ Drug A (n_BA) Reverse Control
Day 0: Drug B Started
→
Independent History
→
Drug A Prescribed

Natural control sequence: Drug A is initiated after Drug B for independent clinical indications.

Statistical Calculations:
• Crude Sequence Ratio (cSR): cSR = n_AB / n_BA — Ratio of observed directional starts.
• Null-effect Ratio (nSR): Accounts for background temporal growth or decay in prescribing volumes over time.
• Adjusted Sequence Ratio (aSR): aSR = cSR / nSR with 95% Clopper-Pearson Confidence Intervals. An aSR > 1.0 indicates Drug B starts after Drug A significantly more often than expected.
• Benjamini-Hochberg (BH) Multiple-Testing Correction: Controls the False Discovery Rate across hundreds of tested drug pairs.

How CASCADE Solves Prescribing Cascades

1. The Healthcare Challenge

Polypharmacy is an urgent patient-safety epidemic. When a patient takes Drug A (e.g., Amlodipine) and develops a side-effect (e.g., ankle edema), a secondary physician mistakes it for a new disorder and prescribes Drug B (e.g., Furosemide). Because doctors work in silos, neither sees the cascade.

2. Loop 1: Counter Interception

At the moment of dispensing, CASCADE scores the prescription against patient medication history in <15ms. An XGBoost model trained on clinical sequence patterns presents the pharmacist with plain-language TreeSHAP factors explaining why the sequence is suspicious.

3. Loop 2: Population Discovery

Streaming Prescription Sequence Symmetry Analysis (PSSA) scans dispensing patterns across all 50 network pharmacies. When medication B starts after medication A significantly more often than the reverse, Loop 2 flags it as a candidate safety signal even if never previously catalogued in literature.

4. Human-in-the-Loop Reporting

CASCADE prepares polite prescriber notes and pre-fills official Indian Pharmacopoeia Commission PvPI suspected adverse drug reaction forms. Crucially, CASCADE generates drafts — it never automatically submits reports without human clinical approval.
